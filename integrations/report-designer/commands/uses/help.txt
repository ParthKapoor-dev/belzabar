Usage: wi-impl rd uses <uuid>

List the reports whose datasource is this AD method: what a change to the
method can break in Report Designer. Read-only.

Arguments:
  <uuid>              The AD method's uuid (draft or published) or its AD URL.

Flags:
  --help, -h          Show this help message.

`wi-impl ad callers <uuid>` covers the methods (and pages) that call it; this covers reports.

Examples:
  wi-impl rd uses 0123456789abcdef0123456789abcdef
