Usage: wi-impl rd datasources [flags]

List what a report can use as its datasource. A datasource is a PUBLISHED AD
method; Report Designer groups them by AD category. Read-only: nothing is run.

Flags:
  --category <name>   List the published methods of this category (name, alias
                      or uuid): chain id, method name, description. Without it,
                      the categories are listed.
  --search <text>     Keep only categories / methods whose name contains this.
  --help, -h          Show this help message.

A method missing here is not published (or not in that category): publish it
with `wi-impl ad publish` before a report can use it.

Examples:
  wi-impl rd datasources --search test
  wi-impl rd datasources --category Reports
  wi-impl rd datasources --category Reports --search invoice
