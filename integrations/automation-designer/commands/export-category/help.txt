Usage: belz ad export-category <category> [flags]

Export an entire category (all methods) via
/rest/api/automation/chain/export/category/{id}. The category may be given
by name, chain-category id or uuid, or automation-system id or uuid; it is
resolved to the chain-category id (the one `belz ad categories` shows as ID).

Flags:
  --out <file>    Write the exported JSON to a file; the envelope then omits
                  the body (`preview`).
  --full          With --out: also include the body in the output.
  --llm           Emit a JSON envelope with {id, category, methodCount, writtenTo, bytes, preview}

Examples:
  belz ad export-category Sandbox --out /tmp/sandbox.json
  belz ad export-category 42 --llm
