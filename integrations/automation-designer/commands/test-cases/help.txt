Usage: wi-impl ad test-cases <uuid> [flags]

List the persistent test cases attached to a method's test suite.

Flags:
  --full   Also give each case's description, inputs (decoded, as in a case file;
           inputExtras when the stored wrapper carries more) and assertions
           ({output, op, expected}, or {expression} for raw SpEL)
  --llm    Emit a JSON envelope with {testSuiteId, testCases[], total}

Examples:
  wi-impl ad test-cases 4a1b2c3d...
  wi-impl ad test-cases 4a1b2c3d... --full --llm
