belz pd publish <pageId> --yes  :->  Publish a PD draft to its PUBLISHED sibling (does NOT deploy)
belz pd publish <pageId> --host <id> [--host <id>] --yes  :->  Publish AND deploy to the given host(s)
belz pd publish <pageId> --landing --yes  :->  Publish as the site's landing page
belz pd publish <pageId> --force --yes  :->  Bypass the PUBLISHED-input draft-guard refusal
