wi-impl pd publish <pageId> --yes  :->  Validate + publish a PD draft to its PUBLISHED sibling (does NOT deploy)
wi-impl pd publish <pageId> --host <id> [--host <id>] --yes  :->  Publish AND deploy to the given host(s)
wi-impl pd publish <pageId> --landing --yes  :->  Publish as the site's landing page
wi-impl pd publish <pageId> --force-validation --yes  :->  Publish despite validator errors on the draft
wi-impl pd publish <pageId> --allow-published --yes  :->  Publish a PUBLISHED page that has no linked draft
